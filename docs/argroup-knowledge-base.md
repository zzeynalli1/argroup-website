## Services

### 1. Fire Protection Systems
- **Passive Fire Protection – Fire Stop Systems**: ensures "fire safety of an object
  (building or structure)" — enables evacuation, emergency access, and prevents
  fire spreading to adjacent compartments even during structural collapse.
  Emphasizes fire compartmentalization.
- **Passive Fire Protection – Fireproofing Systems**: "detailed and early planning
  of fire barriers is necessary." Team has "passed the necessary training and
  certification" (no certification names given). Covers detection, design,
  implementation of tested/optimized partition systems.
- **Protecting Cables – Protecting the Environment** (published 2023-09-25):
  cable lifecycle/sustainability angle. Cable materials: metallic (copper,
  aluminum conductors/sheathing) and polymer (PVC, PA, PU, PE, PTFE/Teflon,
  rubber, silicones, XLPE). Europe copper recycling ~45%, aluminum ~30%.
  Strategy: extend cable service life to reduce waste (vs. incineration/landfill).
  States commitment to "sustainability and reducing our own and our end-users
  impact on the environment."

### 2. Testing and Measurement Services
- **Pull Out Test**: non-destructive test correlating pull-out force (via
  specific-geometry test equipment) to concrete compressive strength.
- **Vibration Test** (published 2023-01-18): mechanical vibration testing method
  to objectively evaluate product performance/quality and durability under
  vibrational stress.

### 3. Construction Services
- **Drilling and Concrete Cutting**: diamond-headed equipment; holes of various
  diameters in concrete; cuts concrete, brick, tiles.
  - Wet cutting: continuous water cooling, ~2 L/m indoors, ~3 L/m outdoors,
    produces wastewater needing collection/removal; unsuitable for interior
    door/window work due to flooding risk.
  - Dry cutting: slower, more labor-intensive, uses industrial dust collectors;
    higher cost but minimal room contamination; preserves finishing materials.
  - Performance depends on tool strength, wall material, cutting depth, air
    temperature/weather.
  - Also offers diamond drilling and wall demolition via industrial vacuums.
- **Sound and Acoustic Insulation Services**: noise = object vibration creating
  pressure waves. Distinguishes sound power (source) vs. sound pressure
  (perceived effect). Two transmission paths: airborne and structure-borne.
  Three control strategies: source control (primary), transmission path
  control, receiver treatment (most complex/expensive). Uses transmission
  loss curves for barrier effectiveness.
- **Dynamic and Static Load Analysis**: load evaluation for support frameworks
  (welded steel and modular components) over a facility's operational life.
  Addresses seismic forces (relevant for Azerbaijan) and thermal-expansion
  stress on pipe runs. Notes regulatory bodies mandate this analysis,
  especially in seismic zones.
- **Support Design** (subtitle: "Support Design and 3D Modelling"): load
  calculations for MEP (Mechanical/Electrical/Plumbing) support structures,
  design aligned to architectural specs, shop drawing preparation. Software:
  AutoCAD, Revit (Autodesk), SolidWorks.
- **Vibration Analysis and Solutions** (published 2023-01-26): measures and
  mitigates vibration from mechanical devices/machinery (chillers, generators,
  engine groups). Engineers measure vibration-prone platforms, simulate via
  analysis software, aim for complete/partial elimination — protects comfort
  and extends equipment life via reduced maintenance frequency.
